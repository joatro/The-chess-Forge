Privacy Policy

Application: Packet Chess
Package: com.packet.chess
Effective Date: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Packet Chess ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, enjoyable chess training experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Packet Chess (the "Application").

By installing and using Packet Chess, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Packet Chess is designed with privacy in mind. The majority of your data remains strictly on your device:

  • Game Progress & Statistics: Your local ELO ratings, completed levels, match statistics, and game history.
  • Customization Preferences: Selected board styles, piece themes, and visual preferences.
  • Profile Photos: If you optionally set a local profile picture, the photo is processed and stored 100% locally on your device. It is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

B. Information Processed via Third-Party Services

Packet Chess uses minimal, secure third-party services to enhance gameplay:

  • Google Play Billing & Play Games Services: Used to handle optional in-app purchases (e.g., hint packages) and game achievements. All transactions are securely processed by Google Play. We do not store or have access to your credit card or financial details.
  • OpenAI API (Optional AI Assistant & Match Analysis): When you ask the AI Assistant for chess advice or request a match summary, board context (e.g., chess FEN notation) and your query are sent securely over HTTPS to the OpenAI API. No personal identifying information (PII) is included in these requests.

C. Advertising & Tracking

  • No Targeted Advertising: Packet Chess does not include advertising SDKs or tracking frameworks.
  • No Advertising ID (AD_ID): The Application explicitly disables Google Advertising IDs (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID).

3. Device Permissions

The Application requests only minimal permissions necessary for core functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for Google Play Billing and AI Assistant queries over secure HTTPS.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely for optional tactile haptic feedback during piece moves and game events.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Packet Chess complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

  • The Application is suitable for users of all ages.
  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions).

5. Data Security & Storage

All external communications (such as AI queries and Google Play transactions) are encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Local game data is managed securely by the Android OS sandbox environment.

6. Data Retention & User Data Deletion

Because all personal configurations and match records are stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete all application data at any time through your device settings: Settings > Apps > Packet Chess > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the Application automatically removes all local data permanently from your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
